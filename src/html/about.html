Mission

MicroBRSoils - A Gateway to Understanding Soil Quality in Brazil

MicroBRSoils is a living, expanding database designed to reveal the biological complexity beneath Brazilian soils. Built within the scope of the CNPq-funded microBRsoil project, it aggregates physical, chemical, enzymatic, and microbiome data to create the foundations of a national Intelligent Soil Quality Index (ISQI). Our long-term vision is to transform raw metataxonomic and metagenomic data into actionable, AI-driven insights for agriculture, supporting sustainable crop production across all Brazilian biomes.

Purpose

Why MicroBRSoils?

Healthy soils underpin resilient agroecosystems. Microbes regulate nutrient cycling, influence plant health, and respond rapidly to environmental and management changes. Yet Brazil still lacks a unified, accessible, microbiome-based soil quality reference.

MicroBRSoils fills this gap by offering:

  • A curated, high-quality reference dataset of Brazilian agricultural and native soils.
  • AI-ready, harmonized metadata following best practices of the Brazilian Microbiome Project.
  • Simple tools for exploration, comparison, and interpretation of your own soil microbiome data.
  • A foundation for precision agriculture, enabling early detection of soil degradation and more informed management decisions.

Initially restricted to internal samples, the platform is being built to soon allow upload and automated analysis of external microbial datasets, supporting farmers, researchers, extensionists, and industry partners.

Technology

Technology Behind MicroBRSoils

The database integrates:

  • Relational SQL backend
  • Python-based analytics
  • Web interface in PHP and modern frameworks
  • Pipelines aligned with BMPOS and international microbiome standards
  • AI models for classification, regression, and pattern detection

This technical foundation matches the infrastructure and methods described in the project (webserver, HPC resources, multi-omics integration, machine learning modeling).

Commitment

Our Commitment

MicroBRSoils is built to be:

  • Scientifically robust
  • Accessible and affordable
  • Continuously updated
  • Open for collaboration
  • Transparent and guided by Brazilian biodiversity regulations (SISGen-compliant)

Our goal is simple: Enable Brazil and the world to understand soil health through the lens of microbial ecology and artificial intelligence.
