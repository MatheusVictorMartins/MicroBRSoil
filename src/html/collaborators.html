Collaboration focus

What this network enables

  • Shared protocols for microbiome and metadata standardization.
  • Cross-biome sampling aligned with Brazilian biodiversity regulations.
  • Accelerated translation of research into agricultural practice.
  • Open access to quality-controlled reference datasets.

Support

Sponsors and Institutional Support

MicroBRSoils is possible thanks to a combination of public funding, scientific collaboration, and private-sector innovation:

Public funding
  • CNPq - National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (Brazil)
  • INCT - Soil Biodiversity (CNPq/CAPES/FAPEMIG)
Private sector
  • Andrios Soil Microbiology Consulting
  • Biome Solutions - Microbial Analytics and Biocontrol

Their support ensures an affordable and scalable platform, without the need for costly user fees during the initial rollout.
